Delete Your Tap Rush Account

Last updated: [20-11-2026]

We respect your privacy and give you control over your personal data. This page explains how you can request deletion of your Tap Rush account and the data associated with it.

1. How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your Tap Rush account directly from within the app:

  1. Open Tap Rush on your Android device.
  2. Sign in using your Google account.
  3. Navigate to the account or settings section.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the on-screen instructions to confirm deletion.

Account deletion removes your Tap Rush authentication account and associated leaderboard entry from our systems.

2. Request Deletion Without Access to the App

If you cannot access Tap Rush or are unable to delete your account through the app, you can submit a deletion request by emailing us:

Email: anantversestudios@gmail.com

Please use the Google account associated with your Tap Rush account when contacting us. Include "Tap Rush Account Deletion" in the subject line. We may ask for reasonable information to verify account ownership before processing the request.

3. What Data Is Deleted?

When your account deletion is completed, we delete the following:

  • Your Tap Rush Firebase Authentication account.
  • Your associated leaderboard entry and scores stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Account-related preferences stored locally by the app.

4. What Data Is Retained?

Tap Rush does not intend to retain personal account information after a completed deletion request, except where retention is necessary to comply with applicable legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

Any data retained for these purposes will be kept only for the period required and handled in accordance with applicable law.

5. How Long Does Deletion Take?

Deletion initiated through the app is processed by the account deletion workflow. Requests submitted by email will be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period, subject to ownership verification where necessary.

6. Guest Users

Players who use Tap Rush without signing in with Google do not have a registered Tap Rush account. Their gameplay data is stored locally on their device, where applicable. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data may remove locally stored gameplay data.
