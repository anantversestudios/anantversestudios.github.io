Privacy Policy for Tap Rush

Effective Date: November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tap Rush handles information when you use the mobile application.

1. Data Stored Locally

Your high score, coin balance, and sound/haptics preferences are stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising & Consent (Google AdMob & UMP)

Tap Rush displays advertisements served by Google AdMob. Depending on your region and consent choices via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP), AdMob may collect or process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. You can update your consent preferences at any time inside the app under Settings → Privacy Settings.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional purchases (such as "Remove Ads") are processed securely by Google Play. Tap Rush does not collect or store your payment card details.

4. Children's Privacy

Tap Rush is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
