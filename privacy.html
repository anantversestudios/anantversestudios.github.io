Privacy Policy

Last updated: [20-11-2026]

This Privacy Policy explains how Tap Rush ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the Tap Rush mobile application ("App").

Tap Rush is a casual mobile game published by Anantverse Studios. We aim to collect only the information needed to provide the game's features, advertising, and leaderboard functionality.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Age Category

When you first use the App, you are asked to select an age category so that the App can provide an appropriate experience.

The App uses age categories to determine which features and advertising settings are available. The categories include child, teen, and adult classifications.

  • We do not ask you to enter your full date of birth.
  • We do not intend to store or transmit your raw age input.
  • Your selected age category is stored locally on your device to apply the appropriate experience.
  • Age category information is excluded from the App's configured backup and device-transfer rules.

1.2 Google Sign-In and Account Information

Adult users can optionally sign in using Google Sign-In. If you choose to sign in, we use Firebase Authentication to authenticate your account.

Depending on the sign-in information provided by Google, this may include your Google account identifier, email address, and profile information made available during authentication.

Google Sign-In is not available to users classified as children or teens in the App.

1.3 Leaderboard Information

Adult signed-in users can participate in the online leaderboard. Information associated with leaderboard participation may include:

  • Firebase user identifier.
  • Player name entered in the App.
  • Game score and leaderboard-related information.

Leaderboard information is stored using Google Firebase Firestore. Player names and scores may be visible to other users through the leaderboard.

Children and teens do not have access to Google Sign-In or the online leaderboard. They can play using the available offline gameplay features.

1.4 Advertising and Device Information

Tap Rush uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Depending on the user's age category, consent status, and applicable requirements, Google and its advertising services may process information such as:

  • Device and app information.
  • Advertising identifiers, where permitted.
  • IP address and general network information.
  • Ad impressions, interactions, and diagnostic information.
  • Advertising consent and privacy choices.

Advertising settings are configured according to the user's age category and applicable requirements. Child users receive child-appropriate advertising settings, and rewarded ads are disabled for child users.

We do not intend to use personalized advertising for users classified as children or for users for whom personalized advertising is not permitted.

2. How We Use Information

Information processed through the App is used to:

  • Provide and maintain gameplay features.
  • Authenticate eligible adult users.
  • Maintain and display the online leaderboard.
  • Display advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Apply age-appropriate features and advertising settings.
  • Manage consent and privacy choices where applicable.
  • Maintain App security, reliability, and performance.
  • Respond to support requests and account deletion requests.

3. Children and Teen Users

Tap Rush is designed to provide a casual gaming experience with age-appropriate restrictions.

Users classified as children or teens cannot use Google Sign-In, submit public player names, or participate in the online leaderboard through the App.

Advertising settings are configured according to the applicable age category. Child users receive child-appropriate ad settings, and rewarded advertising is disabled for them.

Where applicable, the App applies additional restrictions for users who are below the relevant age of consent.

4. Third-Party Services

Tap Rush uses services provided by Google to support authentication, advertising, leaderboard functionality, and consent management.

  • Google AdMob: Used to display advertisements. Google Advertising Policies and Information .
  • Firebase Authentication: Used to authenticate eligible adult users.
  • Firebase Firestore: Used to store online leaderboard information.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP): Used to manage advertising consent and privacy choices where applicable.

These third-party services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security Information .

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by our service providers, including Google services, to provide authentication, advertising, consent management, and leaderboard functionality.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or where necessary to protect the security and rights of users and the App.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Account and leaderboard information for eligible adult users is stored using Firebase services.

Local gameplay information may be stored on the user's device. The age category is stored separately from ordinary app preferences and excluded from the configured backup rules.

We retain account and leaderboard information for as long as necessary to provide the relevant services, or until the user deletes their account, subject to applicable legal requirements.

7. Account and Data Deletion

Adult users can delete their Tap Rush account from within the App using the Delete Account option.

Account deletion removes the associated Firebase Authentication account and the user's corresponding leaderboard entry from Firestore, along with applicable local account preferences.

If you cannot access the App, you can request account deletion by following the instructions on our Account Deletion page .

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through Tap Rush. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal information.

Eligible users can manage available advertising privacy and consent choices through the options presented in the App, where supported.

You can also contact us using the details below for questions or requests relating to your personal information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our services, or applicable legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated effective date.
